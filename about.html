Our
story.

We opened Daud in 2015 with one goal: build the running shop we'd always wanted. A decade in, we still hand-pick every shoe, layer and accessory for runners just like us.

Since
2015the first 6 a.m. batch

We're all about the run.

Whether you're after a 5K or 42.2 km, on road or trail, we're here to prepare, empower and equip you, from your first training run to race day. Our tried-and-tested range covers road, trail, track, apparel and accessories, with zero compromise on performance or style.

Daud is small and independent, but the community around it is not. We run a Sunday club, host free gait analysis, and put 20% of profits from kids' kits into shoes for school athletes.

Whatever kilometre you're on, we want to be your shop. Come say hello.

Maria and Kshitij, founders

Three things we won't compromise on.

Curated, not sold to us.

Every brand we carry has been tested, worn and vouched for by our team. If it can't survive a monsoon long run, it doesn't hit our wall.

Community over transaction.

Free gait analysis, a Sunday run club and race-day support. Come in for shoes, stay for the kilometres. Ask for Maria and she'll lace you up right.

Craft in every detail.

From how we lay out the store to how we pack an order, the small stuff matters. We've spent a decade sweating the details so your run is smoother.

How we got here.

  1. 2015

    Doors open.

    Meera opens a 40 sq m shop in Indiranagar with 3 shoe brands and a lot of hope.

  2. 2018

    The clinic launches.

    Free gait analysis and injury consults every Saturday, booked out within weeks.

  3. 2022

    Sunday run club.

    6 a.m., rain or shine. Started with 6 runners, now averages 80+.

  4. 2026

    40 brands and counting.

    Ten seasons in, we still hand-pick every product. Same team, same goal.
